# Cave of Seals loot — a new table

Status: **PROPOSED, nothing built.** 2026-08-26, against v1.10.91.

---

## 1. What is wrong with the shipped table

Measured, not asserted — the current pools decoded through the IPS-patched ROM.

### 1a. Two of the three floors sell you what the shop already sells

The valley's purchasable ceiling is **Kazus, Mithril gear at 500 G**:

```
ur_weapon   Dagger 60  Long 100  Staff 40  Nunchuck 60  Bow 100
ur_armor    Leather 95/40/15  Copper 80
kazus_weapon  Mithril 400 / 500 / 500
kazus_armor   Mithril 350 / 180 / 130 / 120 / 120
```

Against that:

| floor | gear it drops | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 2000 | Long 100, Leather 95, Copper 80, Dagger 60 | **below Ur's shop** |
| 2001 | Mithril rod 400, Carapace helm 450, Mithril knife 500, Mithril sword 500 | **exactly Kazus's shop** |
| 2002 | Mithril sword 500, Tonfa 500, WSlayer 1000, Carapace body 1250 | the only floor with anything new |

A chest on floor 1 of the SECOND dungeon hands you gear you outgrew before you
left Ur. There is no reason to open it.

### 1b. The two best items in the valley are ~9% coin flips

`WSlayer` (1000 G) and `Carapace body` (1250 G) sit at 9.2% each on floor 3, in a
dungeon whose chests **deliberately skip the server's replay gate** because it
regenerates per entry (CLAUDE.md, chest loot). They are farmable.

### 1c. A 3000 G consumable is farmable

`FenixDown` — 3000 G, twenty times the most expensive thing Ur sells — appears on
every floor and its rate *rises* with depth: 3.2% → 4.1% → 5.1%. In a
replay-exempt dungeon that is the valley's largest economy hole.

### 1d. The table has no idea what dungeon it is in

⭐ **Every encounter in the Cave of Seals is UNDEAD** — Mummy, Skeleton, Shadow,
Laruwai, CurseCoin, Revenant, all `weakness: [fire, holy]`. The cartridge says so
in script `0x23b`:

> "The Sealed Cave is guarded by undead monsters. They may be defeated by
> casting Cure!"

And the boss is the mirror image: `Jinn`, weak to **ice**, resists fire, and his
only attack IS Fire.

So the dungeon asks three questions — *fire* and *holy* on the way down, *ice* at
the bottom, and *don't get burned* when you arrive. **The current loot answers
none of them.** It is the Altar Cave's ladder with bigger numbers.

### 1e. Two smaller things

- **Mimic is flat ~12.5%** on all three floors — the one outcome that does not
  scale with depth.
- **Map 2003, the boss floor, has no pool.** `DEFAULT_LOOT` is `LOOT_POOLS[1000]`,
  so a chest there would roll the OPENING dungeon's floor-1 table, mimic
  included. Probably unreachable; nothing asserts it.

### 1f. What the encounters already cover

`Laruwai`, `Shadow` and `Revenant` already drop `Eyedrop` / `Antidote` at the
ROM's rate 1 (14.3%). **Chests should not duplicate cheap status cures** — that
supply already exists.

---

## 2. Principles for the new table

1. **A chest never offers what a valley shop stocks.** Below the 500 G ceiling it
   pays gil instead.
2. **The loot answers the dungeon's own questions.** Fire and holy on the way
   down, fire *protection* before the boss.
3. **The best things are earned, not rolled.** The trophy weapon comes off the
   boss; the deepest floor holds the second-best.
4. **Nothing worth 3000 G in a farmable pool.**
5. **Mimic scales with depth.** It is the dungeon getting meaner, not a constant.
6. **The boss floor is explicit**, not an inherited default.

---

## 3. The Djinn drops the WSlayer

⭐ **`WSlayer` (0x25) is a HOLY sword, atk 15, 1000 G** — the anti-undead weapon,
currently found on floor 3 **of the undead dungeon it exists to clear**. You
receive the tool after you have finished needing it.

As the boss drop it becomes a trophy that leaves with you.

### Where it has to live

⛔ **NOT in `data/monsters.js`.** That file is auto-generated by
`gen-monsters-js.js` and hand-edits vanish on the next regeneration (CLAUDE.md).

⛔ **NOT as a ROM drop table.** FF3 gives bosses **rate 0** — 52 monsters carry a
drop table and neither the Land Turtle nor the Djinn is among them.
`check-drop-roll.mjs` pins that ("only the monsters that can actually drop carry
a table"), and it is right to.

⛔ **The drop roll never sees a boss anyway.** `battle-update.js` loops over
`battleSt.encounterMonsters`; a boss fight has no such array — it runs on
`getEnemyHP`/`setEnemyHP`.

⭐ **Proposal: a `bossDrop` field on the DUNGEON REGISTRY row.** `data/dungeons.js`
is already "one row per dungeon, and the only place a dungeon's identity is
written down", already carries `bossId`, is hand-maintained and imports nothing:

```js
{
  id: 'seals',
  bossId: 0xCD,
  bossDrop: { item: 0x25, rate: 2 },   // WSlayer, 2-in-7 (28.6%)
  ...
}
```

`rate` reuses the ROM's own ladder (`rate/7`, the same `DROP_GATE_DIE` the
encounter roll uses) so there is one drop-chance idiom in the codebase, not two.
Granted on the boss-death path beside `noteBossDefeated`, through the same
server-validated channel as a quest reward.

⚠ **Rate is the open question.** 2/7 means most players do not see it on their
first kill; the boss respawns on world-map exit, so it is farmable. The
alternative is guaranteed-first-kill-then-never, which needs a flag. **Joel's
call.**

## 6. Gates

1. ⭐ **No chest may hold what a valley shop stocks below its own price.** The
   rule §1a violates today; a gate makes it permanent.
2. **No pool holds an item above a per-dungeon value ceiling** — catches the next
   FenixDown before it ships.
3. **Every dungeon floor that can hold a chest has its own pool.** Fails if a
   floor would fall through to `DEFAULT_LOOT`.
4. **Mimic rate is monotonic with depth** within a dungeon.
5. **The boss drop is granted through the server-validated channel**, once, and
   the item is real.
6. ⭐ **Every scroll obtainable in the valley is tier 1** — the gate discussed
   when Ice2 came up. Nothing stops a future pass dropping tier-3 magic into
   `LOOT_POOLS[1000]` today.

## 7. Open questions

1. **The boss-drop rate** — 2/7 farmable, or guaranteed-once behind a flag? (§3)
2. **Sell price.** Removing FenixDown takes ~150 G of expected sell value out of
   each floor-3 chest and Carapace body puts a similar amount back. Whether that
   is neutral depends on the shop BUY-BACK fraction, which this plan has not
   measured.
3. **Holy arrows need a bow.** Ur sells one for 100 G. If bows are a dead end in
   practice the arrow tier should become something else — worth a look at whether
   anyone actually equips one.
4. **Does the Altar Cave need the same pass?** Its table has the same shape and
   was the model this one copied. Not measured here.
